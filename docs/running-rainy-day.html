Rainy Day Merchandise

Running your shop

Step-by-step instructions for adding products, handling orders, and changing the website.

Guide updated 5 October 2026

Add a product

Products are added in Square, never in HubSpot. The website picks them up by itself within about five minutes.

  1. Sign in to your Square Dashboard on a computer and open Items & inventory, then Items. Depending on your account, that first menu may be called Items & services or Items & menus instead.
  2. Click Create item.
  3. Type the name, price and description. The website shows them exactly as you type them, so check the spelling.
  4. Add photos. The first photo is the one shoppers see in the shop. The product page shows up to four.
  5. Choose a category: Bracelets, Charm Bracelets, Earrings, Keychains or Necklaces. The category decides which shop page the item appears on. An item with no category only shows up under Shop All. If you pick more than one, Square asks for a reporting category, and that's the one the website uses.
  6. If you only have a few of it, switch on stock tracking and enter how many you have. See Keep stock counts right below.
  7. Click Save.
  8. Wait about five minutes, then find the item on the website to check it looks right.

One price per item. Square lets you add sizes or options to an item (it calls them variations), but the website only ever sells the first one. If something comes in two sizes, create two separate items.

Change a product

Open the item in Square, change what you need, and click Save. New prices, names, descriptions and photos reach the website within about five minutes.

A category's photo in Square doubles as the banner on that category's page. The Cover photos section on the website tab explains the exceptions.

Keep stock counts right

Stock tracking is what stops the website selling something you've run out of.

  1. Open the item in Square.
  2. In its stock section, switch tracking on and enter how many you have.
  3. Click Save.

Every sale takes one off the count. At zero the website shows Out of Stock and won't let anyone buy it, even someone who already had the page open. When you make more, open the item and raise the count.

Items without tracking can be bought again and again. On 5 October, 17 of your 178 items had tracking switched on. Switch it on for anything you only have one or a few of.

Very rarely, two people buy the last one at the same moment and both orders go through. If that happens, refund one of them. The Orders tab shows how.

Stop selling a product

For now

If you'll make more, switch on stock tracking and set the count to 0. The website shows it as Out of Stock until you raise the count.

For good

Delete the item in Square. It disappears from the website within about five minutes.

Don't use Archive or the Online channel switch to hide an item. The website doesn't reliably follow those settings, so the item can stay for sale.

The item called Shipping

You'll see an item called Shipping in your item list. Leave it there: its price is your shipping fee, and the website keeps it out of the shop. The Tax & shipping tab has the details.

When an order comes in

HubSpot emails you. Everything you need to ship the order is in HubSpot, where each order is called a deal.

  1. Open the new-order email from HubSpot and click through to the deal. You can also find it under CRM → Deals. New orders are in the first column, named Order followed by a number.
  2. Read what to send. Order Items lists what they bought. Shipping Address is where it goes. Amount is what they paid, including tax and shipping. The customer's email and phone number are on the contact in the right-hand panel.
  3. Pack the order and ship it.
  4. Mark the order shipped in Square. The steps are in the next section.
  5. Email the customer their tracking number from rainydaymerchandise@gmail.com. The website tells customers to expect a tracking number by email, and nothing sends it automatically.
  6. Optional: in HubSpot, drag the deal into the Closed won column. That way the first column only holds orders you still need to ship.

New orders land in a column HubSpot calls "Appointment scheduled". That's HubSpot's default name and means nothing for your shop. You can rename it, for example to "New order", without breaking anything:

HubSpot → Settings → Objects → Deals → Pipelines → click the first stage's name

Mark an order shipped in Square

  1. In your Square Dashboard, open Orders & payments, then Orders, then Shipments. On some accounts the first menu is called Payments or Invoices & payments.
  2. Click To-do and open the order.
  3. Click Actions, then Mark as shipped.
  4. Enter the tracking number, choose the carrier, and click Mark as shipped.

Type the tracking number before you confirm. Once an order is marked shipped, Square won't let you change its tracking number, carrier or address.

Questions from customers

Finding their order

Order numbers start with ORD-. Search HubSpot for the number the customer gives you: the deal is named "Order" followed by it. If that finds nothing, search for their name or email instead. Older orders showed customers a different number.

"I didn't get a confirmation email"

The website emails every customer a confirmation right after they pay. If theirs didn't arrive, ask them to check their spam folder. Then check the address on the order: open the deal and look at the contact in the right-hand panel. If the address is wrong, fix it as described next.

They gave the wrong email address

The order itself is fine: it's paid and the shipping address is right. Only the email is wrong, so the customer didn't get their confirmation and won't see the order when they sign in. They can't fix this themselves, so the confirmation page asks them to email you.

  1. Find the order in HubSpot and open the contact in its right-hand panel.
  2. Search your HubSpot contacts for the customer's correct address. What you find decides which of the two fixes below to use.

No contact has the right address

On the contact from step 1, click the email address, change it to the correct one, and save.

One already does

Open the contact with the correct address. Click Actions, then Merge, search for the wrong-address contact, select it, and click Merge. The order moves onto the right contact.

Then email the customer from rainydaymerchandise@gmail.com with their order details. The deal's Order Items and Square Receipt URL have everything they need.

Refund an order

  1. In your Square Dashboard, open Transactions and click the customer's payment.
  2. Click •••, then Issue Refund.
  3. Tick the items you're refunding, or click Refund Amount to refund part of the total. Choose a reason.
  4. Click Issue Refund.

The money usually reaches the customer within a week, though it can take up to two. Square keeps its card fee on refunded payments.

If the item comes back and you track its stock, check its count afterwards and add it back if Square hasn't.

Cover photos

Banner images work differently depending on the page.

The Shop All page

Open the Shop page, click the Category Banner module, and use the Shop All Cover Photo field. Shop All isn't a Square category, so nothing you change in Square affects it.

A category page, such as Bracelets or Earrings

Set the category image in Square. The banner picks it up by itself, and this is the normal way to change one.

To override Square for a single category, add an entry under Category Custom Content and type the Category Name exactly as it is in Square: Bracelets, not bracelets.

A category with no photo in Square

Use the Fallback Cover Photo field, or the banner stays empty. Keychains has no Square photo today.

Theme settings

Under Design → Themes you'll find Brand Color, which you can change freely, and Square Environment.

Leave Square Environment set to "production". Switching it to sandbox points your live shop at Square's test system. Orders would look successful and no money would arrive.

Sales tax

Kansas customers are charged tax. Customers in other states are not, because you don't owe tax elsewhere until you sell a great deal into that state.

Square → Settings → Account & Settings → Payments → Sales taxes

Your rule is called Kansas Jewelry. Change the percentage there and the website uses the new rate straight away.

The rate is currently 6.5%, which is the Kansas state rate on its own. Your county and city add more on top, so at 6.5% you collect less than you owe and make up the difference at filing time. Confirm your full combined rate with whoever does your taxes, then update it here.

Shipping

The shipping fee is the price of the item called Shipping in your Square item list. Change that price to change the fee.

Customers never see it as a product. The website hides it and shows the amount as "Shipping" at checkout. It's one flat charge per order, not per item, so a two-bracelet order pays it once.

Don't delete the Shipping item. It looks like a stray product in your list, but it's where the fee is stored. Deleting it quietly makes shipping free.

What changed

Each update to the shop, newest first.

5 October 2026

Order confirmation emails, and mistyped email addresses caught before payment.

New

Order confirmation emails

Customers get an email right after they pay, listing what they bought, the total, where it's shipping, and a link to their Square receipt. When they reply, it comes to rainydaymerchandise@gmail.com.

Fixed

Mistyped email addresses

An address like name@gmail.con used to go through checkout, and HubSpot then refused it, so the order never reached you. The site now catches it before payment, and at sign-in, and suggests a fix for common slips like gmial.com.

New

Order numbers that match

The order number customers see now matches the one in HubSpot and in their email, so you can search for it. The Orders tab also has steps for fixing an order placed with the wrong email address.

Fixed

Favorites and the cart

The account page shows favorites by name and photo instead of a product code, and the cart no longer says "uncategorized" under items.

2 October 2026

Order history for customers, safer favorites, and an About page.

New

Order history

Signed-in customers can see their past orders on their account page: what they bought, the total, where it shipped, and a link to the receipt.

Fixed

Favorites belong to the customer who saved them

Anyone who knew a customer's email address could see or change that customer's saved favorites. Now favorites are tied to the signed-in customer. Visitors who aren't signed in still have favorites, kept in their own browser.

New

About page

A new About page with sections for your story, testimonials, and a newsletter sign-up.

31 August – 1 September 2026

The shop moved to new hosting, four things that were quietly broken got fixed, and five features were added.

Fixed

Orders never reached HubSpot

Not one order had ever created a record, since the shop was built. The website was trying to save details into fields that didn't exist, HubSpot rejected every attempt, and the error was being thrown away silently. This is why you couldn't see orders, addresses, or customer contact details. Orders now arrive with what was bought, where it ships, and a link to the receipt.

Fixed

Anyone could set their own price

The checkout accepted whatever total the browser sent, so a $200 order could have been paid as one cent. Square now prices every order from your catalog and the site charges what Square says.

Fixed

The menu sometimes came up empty

Your categories loaded in one batch that wasn't big enough, so a visitor arriving at a quiet moment could see a shop with no navigation at all.

Fixed

The Shop All cover photo

The page you told us about. It had no way to be changed. The website tab shows where its field now lives.

New

Kansas sales tax

Kansas customers are charged tax; other states aren't. You control the rate in Square.

New

Shipping fee

A flat charge per order, set by you in Square.

New

Sold out actually means sold out

Out-of-stock items can no longer be bought, not from a stale page and not by anyone poking at the site directly. Works on items where you've switched tracking on.

New

Shipping addresses in Square

Orders now carry the delivery address, so Square's Shipments list shows you where each one is going.

New

Order details in HubSpot

Each sale creates a deal listing the items, the shipping address, and the total, and HubSpot emails you when one arrives.

Under the hood

New hosting

The part of the shop that talks to Square moved to Cloudflare. It's faster, has no slow first load, and is on a plan that permits selling.

Under the hood

Automatic checks

Automated tests run on every change, and updates deploy themselves once they pass. Any change can be undone in about two minutes.
